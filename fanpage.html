About him:

Jordan Terrell Carter, known professionally as Playboi Carti, is an American rapper. Known for his eccentric vocal style and fashion, he is considered an influential figure in modern hip-hop and a pioneer of the rage microgenre.

Interesting facts about playboi carti

01 - A$AP Rocky

Carti met A$AP Rocky at SXSW in 2015, leading to a connection with Rocky's AWGE creative circle.

02 - 00pium

Carti founded Opium, a creative label associated with artists such as Ken Carson and Destroy Lonely.

03 - Rick Owens

Carti is heavily influenced by Rick Owens and his dark, avant-garde fashion aesthetic.
